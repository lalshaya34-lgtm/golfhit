Tutorial — Perfect Orbit Style

This version uses an orbit-style course: aim by dragging from the ball, release to shoot. Train to increase Power and Accuracy — higher Power = farther, higher Accuracy = less deviation.

Skins are cosmetic. Coins are awarded for good shots and finishing holes. Open Train to run drills.
